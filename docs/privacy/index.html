SAOLA Wildlife Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026 · App: SAOLA · Developer: Saola Wildlife

SAOLA is a free wildlife exploration app. You can browse country packs, search species, mark species as seen, make groups and plan approximate routes. No account is required. The app does not request your device location.

Information kept on your device

Your seen-species list, imported names, custom groups, preferences and downloaded country packs are stored locally. The app does not upload your personal species list or groups to us. You can correct individual seen species within the app, delete app data in Android settings, or export a backup through the Android file picker to a destination you choose. Android may include some local app data in your device or Google backup, depending on your settings; the destination and retention of an exported backup depend on your chosen storage provider.

Network requests

Map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap. Country-pack metadata and files are requested from GitHub. A custom HTTPS catalog receives requests only if you configure it. To plan a driving route, the selected stop coordinates are sent to the FOSSGIS routing service. The personal seen-species list is not sent. Newer country packs include compact portraits that load offline. When photos are enabled and a portrait is missing from a pack, iNaturalist may receive requests for scientific names in visible list rows, and its photo hosts may receive image requests. You can turn photos off in Preferences. Those service providers can receive technical network information such as your IP address and may keep their own logs under their policies. Route stop coordinates describe proposed wildlife areas; they are not read from your device GPS.

Purpose, control and retention

Network requests supply map tiles, country data, route calculations and optional photos. Local data supports your personal list and groups. SAOLA has no ads, analytics SDK, in-app purchases or account system. We do not sell your personal data. We do not run a server that stores your personal list. Local data remains until you remove it, clear app data or uninstall the app. External service log retention is controlled by each provider. If you email us, we use that message to answer your question and retain the correspondence as needed to handle it.

Services and their policies

  • OpenStreetMap Foundation (map tiles)
  • FOSSGIS routing service (selected route stops)
  • GitHub (pack distribution and public policy page)
  • iNaturalist (optional photos)
  • Google (Play distribution and optional Android backup)

Your choices and questions

You can use the app without photo requests or driving routes. You can remove seen-species entries in the app. For privacy questions or requests about messages you sent us, contact hello@saolawildlife.com. We may update this policy when the app changes and will update the date above.
